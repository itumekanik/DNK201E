📖 Theoretical Solution — Example 17.3

🔹 Problem Statement

Bar AB rotates with a clockwise angular velocity of \( \omega_{AB} = 10 \text{ rad/s} \). Determine the vertical velocity \( v_R \) of the rack of the rack-and-pinion gear. System given parameters at the initial snapshot:

  • \( A = (0, 0) \), \( B = (6, 12) \), \( C = (22, 10) \), \( D = (22, 0) \) in inches.
  • Pinion touches the rack at radius \( r_{gear} = 6 \text{ in} \).

🔹 General Kinematic Equations

The velocities of pins B and C are fundamentally coupled by the relative velocity equation:

$$ \vec{v}_B = \vec{\omega}_{AB} \times \vec{r}_{B/A} $$ $$ \vec{v}_C = \vec{v}_B + \vec{\omega}_{BC} \times \vec{r}_{C/B} $$

Because C is rigidly attached to the pinion which pivots at D:

$$ \vec{v}_C = \vec{\omega}_{CD} \times \vec{r}_{C/D} $$

🔹 Solution

Given \( \vec{\omega}_{AB} = -10 \hat{k} \) rad/s. Then: $$ \vec{v}_B = (-10 \hat{k}) \times (6\hat{i} + 12\hat{j}) = 120\hat{i} - 60\hat{j} \text{ in/s} $$

For link BC: $$ \vec{r}_{C/B} = (22 - 6)\hat{i} + (10 - 12)\hat{j} = 16\hat{i} - 2\hat{j} $$ $$ \vec{v}_C = (120\hat{i} - 60\hat{j}) + (\omega_{BC}\hat{k} \times (16\hat{i} - 2\hat{j})) $$ $$ \vec{v}_C = (120 + 2\omega_{BC})\hat{i} + (-60 + 16\omega_{BC})\hat{j} $$

For link CD: $$ \vec{r}_{C/D} = (22 - 22)\hat{i} + (10 - 0)\hat{j} = 10\hat{j} $$ $$ \vec{v}_C = \omega_{CD}\hat{k} \times 10\hat{j} = -10\omega_{CD}\hat{i} $$

🔹 Conclusion

Equating components of \( \vec{v}_C \): $$ y\text{-dir}: -60 + 16\omega_{BC} = 0 \implies \omega_{BC} = 3.75 \text{ rad/s} $$ $$ x\text{-dir}: 120 + 2(3.75) = -10\omega_{CD} \implies \omega_{CD} = -12.75 \text{ rad/s} $$

The rack is vertically driven by the right edge of the gear. $$ \vec{v}_{Rack} = \vec{\omega}_{CD} \times \vec{r}_{edge} = (-12.75 \hat{k}) \times (6 \hat{i}) = -76.5 \hat{j} \text{ in/s} $$

$$ v_{Rack} = 76.5 \text{ in/s } (6.375 \text{ ft/s}) \ (\downarrow) $$
